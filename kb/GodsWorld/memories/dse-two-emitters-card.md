The SAME apparatus with a SECOND point source, separated from the first by exactly d = λ_eff/2 — the strongest-coupling case, since two sources reinforce when their separation is a multiple of λ/2 and the coupling falls as (λ/d)². The plate stays at the same leg D = λ_eff/4 from the emitter plane, with the same chart registered to it. Because each source lays its own band a quarter-wavelength above and below itself, and the two sources sit a half-wavelength apart, source 1's LOWER band and source 2's UPPER band land on the SAME line — the midline y = 200, equidistant from both emitters. So the chart should show a CENTRAL peak where the one-emitter rung has none, flanked by the two outer bands at y = 40 and y = 360. That contrast — no centre with one source, a doubly-reinforced centre with two — is the whole claim, and it is one glance at two graphs.
