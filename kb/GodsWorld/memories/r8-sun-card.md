## What you are seeing

What you are seeing: Put light in the scene and let light do what light does: a blazing sun, its glow and god rays spreading in every direction through the space around it.

## Why it is thought to be classically impossible

Why it is impossible the usual way: Games paint glow and god rays onto the finished picture as a separate screen effect, aimed from where the sun is on the screen rather than from any light in the scene.

## How FRQTL gets it

How FRQTL gets it: One sphere emitter, shining in every direction. The god rays are rendered from the density of the particles themselves. No Standard Model, no equations, and the engine does not know whether you are looking at a star, a candle flame or an atom.
