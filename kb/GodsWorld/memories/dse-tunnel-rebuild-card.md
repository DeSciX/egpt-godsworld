## The rung

Rung Double Slit (Tunnel, symmetric) (DSE): The double-slit REBUILT ON THE TUNNEL OBJECT at PHYSICAL DSE PROPORTIONS: the detector sits at the SYMMETRIC distance — emitter-to-front of the slit wall EQUALS back of the slit wall-to-detector. Each slit channel is a DIRECTED Tunnel: a hit on a channel wall ejects the quantum back into the bore and re-emits it in a uniformly random direction within 180 degrees of the corridor axis, so each slit acts as a semi-circular emitter — the classical picture of what a slit does to a wavefront — rather than a mirror that would only ever project the beam it was given. The exit angle is drawn with real trig, so the fan is flat, not diagonal-weighted; the barriers + screen are v2 Walls (destroy / detector preset) so the scene survives Restart. The source emits FORWARD over 180 degrees (the hemisphere preset) rather than all the way round, so the burst that used to fly backward into the catcher now lights the aperture — the same sampler, restricted to the forward half, aimed at the wall. In 2D a balanced, central-dominant three-lobe interference pattern builds up.
