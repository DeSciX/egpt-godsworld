The first true routing demo: a LASER — a genuinely collimated +x beam (the tightest emission the emitter supports: pure +x momentum, a single charge, no wavelength-scale spreading) — fires into a rotatable mirror. The mirror compiles the real reflection law: a surface tilted to θ deflects the beam by 2θ (standard optics), so at 45° the thin beam exits straight up. ROTATE the mirror (the orientation slider, or LOOK-x in 3D) and the thin reflected beam steers live.
