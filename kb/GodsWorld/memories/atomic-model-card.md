## What you are seeing

What you are seeing: Quarks pack into a proton and stay bound, and an electron keeps its place in the atom around it. Stable orbits, without quantum numbers. Like the Big Bang, this simulation is not meant as an assertion of what the correct scales are; it is an exploration of how emergence and scale are related - the engine lets you set stable containment, or experiment with scale so that stability is emergent.

## Why it is thought to be classically impossible

Why it is impossible the usual way: Classical physics says an orbiting electron radiates its energy away and spirals into the nucleus in about 16 trillionths of a second. Textbook physics avoids that only by assuming quantised energy levels.

## How FRQTL gets it

How FRQTL gets it: Frames nested three layers deep: quarks in a proton, the proton and an electron in an atom. Each parent draws its children one step toward its centre every tick, and binding emerges from that. No force equation.
