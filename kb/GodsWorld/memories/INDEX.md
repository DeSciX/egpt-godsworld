## How a scene's learning grows

A card is the registry's text and is rewritten from it on every run; nothing learned is written into it. A scene's learning grows by seen memories: <id>-seen.md for the scene, and the Record memories below, each published from a run on one scene (its `scene` column) and extending that scene's card, <scene>-card.md. What Maxi learned is written as a seen memory that names the scene, never as a copy of the card's text, so a question about a scene finds its card and then what was learned in it.

## Record memories

A Record memory is what a loop run built from a person's outline and published from its complete Record; it is outside the scene roster. Its Record (clips, stills, record.json, lab.json, parts, project/) and the roles' answers (turns/) are served under site/memories/<id>/.
