## What you are seeing

What you are seeing: One burst fills the space, and structure emerges on its own. This is a fractal at its heart - it could be quantum emergence or large cosmic structures ... one set of rules from which attractive forces are a matter of your chosen unit of scale. This simulation is not meant as an assertion of what the correct scales are; it is an exploration of how emergence and scale are related.

## Why it is thought to be classically impossible

Why it is impossible the usual way: Large-scale cosmic structure requires gravitational attraction tuned over 13.8 billion years, and a simulation has to compute that gravity across the whole span.

## How FRQTL gets it

How FRQTL gets it: One point source, and a world that builds larger frames from the top down. No gravity law, no cosmological constant, no equations.
