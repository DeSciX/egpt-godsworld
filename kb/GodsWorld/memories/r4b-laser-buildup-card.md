A laser, assembled from parts you have already met. A GAIN SOURCE — rung 3's omni emitter, restricted to a forward half-fan — fires into a RESONANT BORE whose walls re-aim every hit forward instead of reflecting it, and the beam leaves through an OUTPUT COUPLER at the far mouth. The coupler has two independent switches: RECENTER collapses every exiting quantum onto the bore's mid-line, making the mouth a single point source; PHASE RESET re-locks the ensemble's oscillation so equal-radius quanta share a phase again. Recentering alone gives you the geometry of rung 4; adding phase gives you its coherence. Sweep the two switches against the wavelength and the bore length: at long wavelengths the mouth is already nearly in phase and the bands appear on their own, while at short ones the fan reads smooth until you switch phase on. Nothing here is a wave equation — it is the same charged walk, aimed and re-timed.
