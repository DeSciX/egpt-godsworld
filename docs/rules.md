# Rules this repo applies

Each line is a rule as Maxi's lab and its seat apply it today. Rulings and their wording are kept in the private godsworld-dev records, never in this repo.

- Maxi's eyes are video only: every reading is a 5 to 10 second MP4 clip filmed through FrqtlVision's mediabunny recorder, a drag runs over ticks inside a recording so its effect is watched, the proof runs on 2D scenes first, nothing stands in for the footage (no stills, numbers or text), memories are whole richly commented sketches with no size limit, and the published frqtl-sdk spec is canonical while every recipe lives here as Maxi's own knowledge (record: godsworld-dev docs/records/2026-10-15-egpt-godsworld-records-moved.md)
- Maxi works as three roles on three threads, each with its own interaction id: the Designer (Maxi, JARVIS to the user's Tony Stark) plans from the memories and speaks to the user, the Builder implements and hands back a clip, and the Owl verifies from the outline, the prediction and the footage alone; the loop needs no human click, the conversation carries words and clips, never code, and a clip reaches Maxi as `media[]` on the page's own question call (record: godsworld-dev docs/records/2026-10-15-egpt-godsworld-records-moved.md)
- The seat that builds Maxi works in godsworld-dev, the R&D playground, and ships to this repo (record: godsworld-dev docs/records/2026-10-15-egpt-godsworld-records-moved.md)
- Maxi follows the sketch-project paradigm with the names `parts`, `exp.declare`, `exp.setDeclared` and `frqtl.json`; the design is docs/concepts/maxi-sketch-projects.md (record: godsworld-dev docs/records/2026-10-15-egpt-godsworld-records-moved.md)
- A seen memory is kept only when a human, the local Owl or the seat's own local agent looked at its stills, named on a `looked: <human|local-owl|seat> <role> on <N> stills of memories/<id>/<clip>.mp4 — <account>` line, the looker named by a role (lowercase kebab words such as lab-owner or godsworld-seat-author), never by a person's name; the platform brain's approve alone writes nothing (owner: scripts/gen-memories-seen.mjs; gate: scripts/test/memories.test.mjs)
